Payment Terms
A non-refundable deposit of 50% is required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon delivery of final files. Final deliverables will not be released until full payment is received. Accepted via bank transfer, PayPal, or Wise.

03
Revisions
Up to 2 rounds of revisions are included within the agreed scope. Each revision must be submitted as a consolidated list. Additional revisions are charged at $50 USD per round. Revisions that fundamentally change creative direction are treated as a new project.

04
Delivery & Timelines
Timelines are contingent on the client providing all assets, feedback, and approvals on time. Delays caused by late client feedback will extend the delivery date. Rush delivery within 48 hours is available at a 30% surcharge.

05
Intellectual Property & Usage Rights
Upon full payment, the client receives full commercial usage rights to the final delivered files. RahulFX3D Studio retains the right to display the work in its portfolio unless requested otherwise in writing. Raw source files remain the property of RahulFX3D Studio.

06
Cancellation Policy
If the client cancels after work commences, the deposit is non-refundable. If cancelled after 50% or more of work is complete, 75% of the total fee is due. If Rahul cannot complete the project, a full refund of payments for undelivered work will be issued.
