Client Kickoff & CGI Guidelines

Studio
Onboarding

Welcome to the workspace. Below is your onboarding blueprint and asset requirements checklist. Please review these details and download/print this sheet for reference.

CGI Production Pipeline 01
Our production follows a structured 5-stage pipeline to ensure maximum visual fidelity and smooth feedback loops.
Phase Focus Client Action / Deliverable
01. Concept & Briefing Moodboards, storyboard block-outs, camera angles. Review moodboard & approve creative direction.
02. 3D Asset Prep Modeling products, package labels, texture mapping. Supply 3D STEP/OBJ models & high-res label files.
03. Lighting & FX Liquid physics simulation, lighting setups, scene details. Review clay/gray-shaded draft renders.
04. Draft Reviews Color-graded animatics, texture iterations. Provide timestamped change logs in review portal.
05. Master Delivery Final rendering farm output, color grading, clean masters. Receive download link upon final invoice clearance.
Required Brand Assets 02
CAD models in STEP, IGES, OBJ, or FBX format. If models are unavailable, we can model from physical dimensions.
High-resolution vector files (AI, EPS, or print-ready PDF) of packaging flat graphics, logos, and typographies.
Color hex codes, fonts, references, or existing product photography to ensure realistic lighting matches.
Master Delivery Formats 03
Apple ProRes 422 HQ or H.264 (MP4) in 4K resolution, matched to specified distribution aspect ratios (e.g. 9:16 vertical & 16:9 widescreen).
Lossless 16-bit PNG or EXR files in ACEScg or sRGB color space, optimized for print and e-commerce web channels.
